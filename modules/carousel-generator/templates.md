# RPD Carousel Templates

These are original RPD template systems, not copies of reference pages.

1. `rpd-editorial` — editorial fashion / magazine hierarchy.
2. `rpd-pink-deal` — RPD pink deal identity with strong price/CTA hierarchy.
3. `rpd-minimal` — clean product-first layout.
4. `rpd-lookbook` — model-led fashion presentation.
5. `rpd-colour-grid` — colour-variant discovery layout.

Reference designs supplied by the RPD owner may influence principles such as hierarchy, pacing, product prominence and CTA placement, but generated layouts must be materially original.
